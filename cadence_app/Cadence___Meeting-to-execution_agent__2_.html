Questions beginners ask

Does Cadence post anything without asking me?

No. Every item goes through a confirmation step where you can edit the owner, the deadline and the wording first.

What is a conflict?

A new decision that contradicts one saved from an earlier meeting, such as a different launch date or database. You choose which one to keep.

Is this connected to a real tracker?

This page is a demo. The final build connects to Jira, Linear or Asana through their APIs.

Can I use my voice or another language?

Yes. Use the mic button to dictate, the language menu to translate labels, and Listen to hear results read aloud.
